TrishulX

An AI-powered, multi-layer cyber defense platform that detects, analyzes and responds to modern threats in real time. Three independent shields work together, with one dashboard on top.

Watches process, file and network behavior with eBPF, scores risk with Isolation Forest, and responds automatically.
